    How to Add Secrets

    Paste Image (Fastest)

    Take a screenshot of the QR code and press Cmd+V (Mac) or Ctrl+V (Win) anywhere in the app.

    Upload QR Code

    Save the QR image and click the image icon in the secret input field to upload and decode it.

    Type Manually

    Type the Base32 secret key manually into the form field.
